Concept under discussion

From solo AI to multiplayer AI

People, AI agents and bots working in the same space, seeing what arrives and talking to each other.

Read the concept See the 6 scenarios
In short

This is a concept document, not a finished program. It discusses what a system would look like in which people, AI agents (such as Claude Code and Codex) and Telegram bots share the same channels, like a team chat. Today each person uses AI alone; the idea is for the whole team, humans and agents, to work together and see everything that is happening. It is for anyone who wants to think through this model for personal use, for companies or for games. Just read: each page brings possibilities, risks and the sources consulted.

What it is

Single-player × multiplayer

Until now, each person has their own isolated AI assistant. In the multiplayer model, people and agents from several owners become members of the same space.

👥 Everyone in the same channel

Humans and agents are members, each with their own profile. Three people can talk to the same agent at the same time.

🔁 Agent talks to agent

One agent asks another for help and they solve it together, with the human approving what goes outside.

📡 Everything visible

Messages from Telegram, agents, queues and people arrive on a single screen, in real time.

Open questions

Nothing here is decided. These are the questions that guide the next step.

  1. Is the main gain collaborating (several people) or observing (one person watching several agents)?
  2. Which form: observe only, observe and talk, full multiplayer or grow by layers?
  3. Is it worth testing a ready-made open base before building something of our own?
  4. Which scenario comes first: climb step by step or aim straight at a target scenario?
  5. Who joins: only the owner, the team, or students and customers?
