# Scenario 3: Company: one department

**Who:** 3 to 10 people from one area (marketing, support, finance), a department lead agent and the personal agents of whoever wants to connect theirs.

**A typical day (example: marketing)**
- 09:00 automatic check-in: the agent asks each person what got done yesterday and updates the kanban.
- The agent warns that an item has been stuck for two days and tags the owner.
- The content routine runs every 6 hours: research, draft, approval in the thread and publishing, as in the video.
- A channel app (post dashboard) shows the stage of each piece and the metrics.

**What shows on screen:** channels by front (`#instagram`, `#newsletter`, `#campaign-x`), apps per channel, listed automations and the department's consumption.

**Possibilities**
- **3a:** the department uses the new tool and connects what already exists (ClickUp, Drive), without recreating anything. This is what the video recommends.
- **3b:** the department just gets an agent inside the chat it already uses (Slack or Teams) [11].
- **3c:** pilot department now, with the idea of taking the model to other areas later.

**Risks:** adoption (automation forgotten after a week, as the video itself warns) and outdated data if nobody marks what they did.

**How to measure success:** stuck tasks going down, shorter approval time and people using it without having to remember.

**Makes sense when:** there is an area with a lot of handoffs and things that "fall through the cracks".
